A factory audit in China before placing a large order should cover legal verification, production capability, quality management systems, compliance and certifications, social/labour conditions, and facility safety. For Australian importers, you’ll also want to confirm export documentation, product-specific standards that align with Australian regulations, and traceability of critical components.
