Key differences at a glance
Aspect	General Factory Audit	Technical Audit
Primary focus	Legitimacy, basic quality system, social compliance, overall capacity	Deep dive into technical capability, process controls, engineering competence, product-specific systems
Standards basis	Simplified ISO 9000-style checklist	Product- and process-specific technical standards, buyer specs, industry norms
Depth	Broad overview (“snapshot”) of supplier	Detailed assessment of how the factory actually makes your product
Typical use	Early-stage supplier screening, basic risk check	Before bulk production of technically complex or high-risk products; when previous quality issues occurred
Who performs it	General auditor / inspection company	Specialist auditor with product/industry technical expertise
When to choose a technical audit

Use a technical audit when one or more of these conditions apply:

Product complexity is high

Items with tight tolerances, functional performance requirements, safety-critical components, or complex assemblies (e.g., electronics, machinery, medical devices, advanced textiles, automotive parts).
