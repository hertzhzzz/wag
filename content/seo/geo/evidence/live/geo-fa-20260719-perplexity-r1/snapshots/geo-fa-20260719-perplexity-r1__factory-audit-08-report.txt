How an Australian buyer can use the report to decide

When reviewing a report for a sourcing decision, focus on:

Can this factory reliably make my product at my required volume and quality? (sections 3–5)

Are there any red flags that could cause legal, reputational, or financial problems in Australia? (section 6)

Are the identified issues fixable within my timeline and budget, and is the supplier willing to act? (section 8)

Is there enough evidence (photos, records, certificates) to support the conclusions? (sections 7 and 10)
