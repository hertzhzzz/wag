## Prompt (exact)
How can I verify the Chinese legal name and Unified Social Credit Code on a supplier's documents?

## User message (UI)
How can I verify the Chinese legal name and Unified Social Credit Code on a supplier's documents?

## Assistant answer (UI text)
To verify a Chinese supplier’s legal name (公司名称) and Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码 / USCC), you should cross-check the supplier’s documents against official Chinese government records and ensure the entity details match exactly.

1. Obtain the supplier’s official documents

Request:

Business License (营业执照)

Must show:

Company legal name (公司名称)

Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码)

Legal representative (法定代表人)

Registered address (住所)

Business scope (经营范围)

Registration authority (登记机关)

Bank account certificate (开户许可证 or bank information confirmation)

The account holder name should match the company legal name.

Export-related documents (if applicable)

Customs registration information

Exporter/foreign trade operator registration records (if applicable)

Tax registration details

Ask for a clear scanned copy with the official company seal (公章).

2. Check the Unified Social Credit Code online

Use China's official enterprise registration database:

National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (国家企业信用信息公示系统)

Search using either:

Unified Social Credit Code

Chinese company name

Verify:

Field	Should Match
Company name	Exact Chinese characters
USCC	Exact 18-digit code
Legal representative	Matches license
Registered address	Matches documents
Company status	Should be "存续" (active)
Registration date	Reasonable business history
Business scope	Relevant to your product
3. Understand the Unified Social Credit Code format

A Chinese USCC has 18 characters:

Example:

91440101MA5XXXXX8A

Structure:

Position	Meaning
1	Registration authority code
2	Entity type
3–8	Administrative division
9–17	Organization identifier
18	Check digit

A supplier should not have:

Missing digits

Altered characters

A code belonging to another company

4. Verify the company name translation

Chinese suppliers often provide an English name that is not legally registered.

Example:

Chinese legal name:

广州XX照明科技有限公司

Possible English names:

Guangzhou XX Lighting Technology Co., Ltd.

The English name is usually not legally binding.

For contracts and payments, use:

Chinese legal company name
+
Unified Social Credit Code

Do not rely only on the English name.

5. Check whether the supplier is actually a manufacturer

The business scope can reveal whether they are a trading company.

Look for terms:

Manufacturer indicators:

生产 (production)

制造 (manufacturing)

加工 (processing)

研发 (R&D)

Trading indicators:

贸易 (trade)

进出口 (import/export)

销售 (sales)

A company with only:

货物进出口
批发
零售

may be a trading company rather than a factory.

6. Match the supplier's bank account

Before payment:

Request:

RMB company bank account details

Bank confirmation letter if possible

Check:

Document	Match
Business license name	✓
Bank account holder	✓
Contract entity	✓
Invoice issuer	✓

Avoid paying:

Personal accounts

Unrelated companies

Hong Kong accounts without explanation

7. Use third-party verification for higher-value purchases

For larger Australian imports, consider:

Chinese corporate credit reports

Factory audits

On-site inspections

Video factory verification

Customs export history checks

A supplier verification report should confirm:

Legal existence

Ownership structure

Registered capital

Litigation history

Administrative penalties

Export capability

Manufacturing capability

Minimum verification checklist for an Australian importer

Before signing a purchase contract:

✅ Business license received
✅ USCC verified on government database
✅ Chinese legal name confirmed
✅ Bank account matches company name
✅ Business scope matches product
✅ Export capability confirmed
✅ Factory/manufacturer status checked
✅ Contract uses Chinese legal entity name

The most important rule: the company name and Unified Social Credit Code on the business license must match the entity receiving payment and signing the contract.
