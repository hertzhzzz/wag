A pre-shipment inspection (PSI) report for an Australian buyer should be a decision‑ready document that clearly shows whether the goods match the contract before final payment is released. In practice, it needs to cover quantity, quality, specifications, packaging/labeling, and include enough evidence (photos, measurements, test results) to support a clear pass/fail/pending recommendation.

How to use the PSI report before releasing payment

Treat the PSI report as the final quality gate: do not authorise final payment until you have reviewed it and are satisfied with the Pass/Pending conditions.

If the report shows Fail or serious Pending issues, use it to:

Negotiate rework at the supplier’s cost.

Arrange a re‑inspection before shipment.

Justify delaying payment under your contract terms.
