Negotiation and resolution options

Based on the severity and type of defects, you typically have five core options:
tradeaiders
+1

Option	When it fits	Key considerations
Rework at factory	Defects are fixable (e.g. cosmetic, assembly, packaging)	Supplier bears cost; re-inspect before shipment; delay timeline
Partial acceptance + discount	Minor defects that don’t affect function or safety	Agree on price reduction reflecting defect rate; document in writing
Replace defective units	Critical/major defects in a portion of the batch	Supplier produces replacements; may split shipment; verify quality again
Cancel and reorder	Systemic quality failure; trust broken	Use deposit leverage; consider alternative supplier; review contract terms
Accept as-is (with risk)	Minor issues; urgent timeline; low impact on end use	Document acceptance; plan for customer communication or warranty buffer

Use your payment terms as leverage – if you still owe the balance (e.g. 70% after 30% deposit), do not release it until the issue is resolved or a written concession is secured.
