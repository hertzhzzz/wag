What evidence should I request to verify that a Chinese supplier can legally export my product?
Completed 2 steps

To verify that a Chinese supplier can legally export your product, ask for the supplier’s business license, export registration or export-capable status, and product-specific compliance documents, then verify each one against official records rather than relying on a PDF alone.
worldfirst
+1

What to request

Business license (营业执照), showing the legal company name, unified social credit code, registered address, legal representative, and business scope.
worldfirst
+1

Export authorization or export-capable status, so you know the company is allowed to ship goods internationally.
worldfirst
+1

Product-specific registration or approval, if your product category needs it; for food exports to China, for example, GACC/CIFER registration rules apply and some categories need official verification.
worldfirst

Certificates and test reports relevant to your product, such as ISO, CCC, CE, RoHS, FDA, or product-specific lab reports, but only if they can be independently verified with the issuer.

Bank account details in the company’s registered name, because mismatches are a major red flag.

How to verify it

Check the business license details against China’s public company registry, such as the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System.
