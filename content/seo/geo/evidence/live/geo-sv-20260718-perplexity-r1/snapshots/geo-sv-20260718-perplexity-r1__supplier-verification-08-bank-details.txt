How should I verify bank account details before paying a new Chinese supplier?

Before paying a new Chinese supplier, verify the bank details through at least two independent channels and make sure the beneficiary name exactly matches the company name on the business license. Treat any request to pay a personal account, a different company name, or a suddenly changed account as a red flag until it is fully explained in writing.
