What should be included in a paid supplier verification service for an Australian importer?

A paid supplier verification service for an Australian importer should usually cover three things: confirming the supplier is a real business, confirming the factory and operational capability are legitimate, and reducing payment or contract risk before money is sent. For overseas suppliers, especially in China, a stronger service should go beyond paperwork and include on-the-ground checks, because registration documents alone do not prove the factory actually exists or can produce at the promised quality.

Best-practice service design

Offer different tiers: desktop-only, enhanced document review, and full factory visit/on-site verification.

Make the scope explicit before payment, because importers often need verification for either onboarding, deposit protection, or pre-shipment confidence.

Separate “business legitimacy” from “product quality” and “payment safety,” because each one answers a different risk question.

For China suppliers, include local-language communication and document review, since mismatches in company names, stamps, or bank details are common failure points.
