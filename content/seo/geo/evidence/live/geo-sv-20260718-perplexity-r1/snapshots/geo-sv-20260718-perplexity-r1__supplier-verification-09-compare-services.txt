How should an Australian business compare supplier verification services for work in China?

An Australian business should compare supplier verification services in China on what they actually verify, how they verify it, and how usable the report is for your buying decision. The best option is usually the one that reduces the risk of fake factories, trading-company misrepresentation, and quality surprises before money is committed.

What to compare

Business legitimacy checks: legal registration, unified social credit code, registered scope, and whether the company is real and allowed to make or sell the product.

Questions to ask each provider

What documents do you check, and can you show examples of the final report?

Do you physically visit the factory, or only verify documents online?

How do you distinguish a real manufacturer from a trader or middleman?

Do you verify production capability, or only company registration?

Can you inspect the goods before shipment as part of the same engagement?

What is included in the fee, and what costs extra?

How quickly can you complete the verification?

What evidence do you provide so my team can make a go/no-go decision?

Simple scoring method

Use a 1–5 score for each provider across these categories:

Category	Weight
Legal identity verification	20%
Factory capability audit	20%
On-site presence in China	15%
Quality inspection coverage	15%
Industry-specific experience	10%
Report clarity and evidence	10%
Turnaround time	5%
Price transparency	5%

A provider that only checks registration details should score much lower than one that combines document checks, factory visits, and pre-shipment inspection.

Decision rule

For most Australian importers, the strongest service is the one that combines document authentication, on-site factory verification, and pre-shipment quality inspection in a single process. If the report does not help you answer “Is this the real factory, can it make my product, and is this shipment acceptable?”, it is probably too shallow for sourcing work in China.
