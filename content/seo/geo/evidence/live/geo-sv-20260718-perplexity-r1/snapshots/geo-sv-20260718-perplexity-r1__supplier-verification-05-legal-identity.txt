How can I verify the Chinese legal name and Unified Social Credit Code on a supplier's documents?

You can verify it by matching the supplier’s Chinese legal name and 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code on the business license against the company’s official registry record. The key is to make sure the legal name, USCC, registration status, and business scope all point to the same legal entity, not just a similar English name.

Practical workflow

Ask the supplier for a clear color copy of the business license.

Extract the Chinese legal name and USCC from that copy.

Cross-check those details against the supplier’s quote, contract, invoice, and bank account name.

Verify that the entity is active and that the business scope matches the transaction.

For higher-value orders, add a factory audit or third-party due diligence check.
