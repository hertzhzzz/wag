An Australian buyer should arrange a During Production Inspection (DPI) rather than waiting for a Pre-shipment Inspection (PSI) when the order involves new suppliers, strict workmanship requirements, tight delivery schedules, or when the order includes multiple styles, colors, sizes, or packaging details. DPI allows buyers to identify quality and production issues early, enabling corrective actions before the full order is completed. This is particularly beneficial for orders with higher risk or tighter delivery requirements, as it provides better visibility and control over quality and production progress.
