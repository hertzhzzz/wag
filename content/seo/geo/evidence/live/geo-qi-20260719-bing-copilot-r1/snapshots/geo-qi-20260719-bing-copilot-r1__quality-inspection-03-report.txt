A pre-shipment inspection report should include the following key components to ensure the buyer can make an informed decision before final payment:
Order Information: Details such as the order quantity, carton count, product model, and production status.
Product Quality Checks: Appearance, workmanship, function, dimensions, and performance checks.
Packaging and Labeling: Integrity, accuracy, and compliance with packaging requirements and labeling standards.
Defect List: A comprehensive list of defects with severity, sample size, photos, and videos when useful.
Clear Conclusion: A clear conclusion such as pass, fail, pending correction, or re-inspection required.
The report should connect findings to the buyer's standard, explaining what was checked, what was not checked, what failed, and what decision the buyer should consider before shipment release. It should also include a procurement brief with product, quantity, standard, packaging, and destination details to ensure all aspects are covered.
