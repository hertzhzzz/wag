Packaging, labelling, and compliance checks should an AU-NZ importer add to a China inspection?
AU-NZ importers should add the following checks to their China inspection process to ensure compliance with local regulations:
Quality Control: Implement a quality control plan that includes inspection types, AQL sampling, and compliance with Australian and New Zealand standards. This helps avoid common QC failures and ensures products meet safety and quality standards.
1
Labelling Compliance: Verify that all products have the correct labelling, including origin statements, safety marks, and retailer expectations. This is crucial for meeting China's stringent labelling requirements.
1
Regulatory Compliance: Ensure that products comply with China's biosecurity, food health, and safety regulations, especially for perishable foods. This includes checking for specific bilateral agreements or protocols for plant and animal origin products.
1
Market Access Requirements: Understand and meet the market access requirements for different product categories, which may include country-level market access approval, establishment registration, and product-specific certification.
1
Pre-shipment Inspection: Conduct pre-shipment inspections to ensure that all products meet the required standards and are ready for export. This includes checking packaging, labelling, and compliance with all necessary regulations.
1
By incorporating these checks, AU-NZ importers can reduce the risk of product failure and ensure that their products are compliant with China's regulations, facilitating a smoother import process.
