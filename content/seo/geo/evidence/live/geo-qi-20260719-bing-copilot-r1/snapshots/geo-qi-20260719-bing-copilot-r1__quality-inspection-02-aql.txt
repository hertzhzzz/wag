To set an AQL standard and defect limits for a China production order, a buyer should follow these steps:
Define Defect Categories: Clearly categorize defects into critical, major, and minor based on the product's intended use and safety.
1
Select Inspection Level: Choose an inspection level based on ISO 2859-1 standards, with Level II being the default for most consumer goods.
1
Agree on Sample Size: Use standardized AQL tables to determine the sample size needed for the inspection.
1
Set AQL Values: Assign AQL values to each defect category, indicating the maximum number of defects allowed to pass the inspection.
1
Document the Plan: Write down the AQL inspection plan, including defect definitions, action plans for failed inspections, and release rules.
1
By following these steps, a buyer can ensure a fair and consistent inspection process for their production orders, minimizing the risk of defective products reaching the market.
