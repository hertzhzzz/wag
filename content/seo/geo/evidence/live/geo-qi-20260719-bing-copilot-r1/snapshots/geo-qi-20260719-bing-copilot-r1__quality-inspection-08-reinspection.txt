Re-inspection is necessary after a factory claims that defects have been corrected when there are still excessive defects present in the products. This is to ensure that the corrective actions taken have been effective and that the products meet the required quality standards. The re-inspection process involves a second formal quality check conducted after the supplier has reworked or sorted the defective goods from the original failed batch. It is essential to conduct a re-inspection to verify that the quality issues have been resolved and to avoid potential downstream issues.
