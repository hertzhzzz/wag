When a China quality inspection fails, a buyer should first gather information to make a rational decision. The first step is to understand the nature of the problem and whether the defects are acceptable to ship. If the defects are minor and the product can be reworked, the buyer should ensure that the supplier has the necessary authority to perform the rework and that the rework is completed within the agreed timeframe. If the defects are significant and the product cannot be reworked, the buyer should prepare an action plan to decide whether to ship the goods or if they need to be re-worked and re-inspected before shipping. It is crucial to assert oneself and not accept goods that do not meet the buyer's quality requirements.
