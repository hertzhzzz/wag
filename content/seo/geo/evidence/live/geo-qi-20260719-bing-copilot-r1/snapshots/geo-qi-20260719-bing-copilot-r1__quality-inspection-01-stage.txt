The Pre-shipment Inspection (PSI) is the recommended stage for Australian importers before goods leave a factory in China. This inspection is crucial as it is the last opportunity to catch any issues before the goods are shipped. The PSI involves an independent third-party inspection where an inspector checks a sample of the finished goods against the specifications, approved sample, and packaging requirements. This inspection is particularly important for ensuring that the factory is using the specified materials and that the production setup matches what was quoted. If the inspection fails, the importer has the leverage to negotiate with the factory for rework, which can prevent significant financial losses.
