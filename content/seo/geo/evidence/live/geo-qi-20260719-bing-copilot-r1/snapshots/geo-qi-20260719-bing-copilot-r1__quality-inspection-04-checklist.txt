To create a product-specific inspection checklist, importers should follow these steps:
Order and Quantity Verification: Confirm the right product arrived in the right quantity.
Packaging and Labeling: Check both the product packaging and the outer shipping carton.
Product Dimensions and Weight: Measure a sample of units against approved specifications.
Defect Definitions: Clearly define what constitutes a defect for the specific product.
Information Inspectors Need: List the information inspectors need before the factory visit.
By using a product-specific checklist, importers can ensure that the inspection process is tailored to their specific requirements, leading to more accurate inspection reporting and fewer disputes.
