PROMPT: What official records should an Australian importer check before engaging a supplier in China?
---
Before engaging a supplier in China, an Australian importer should check the following official records:
Business Registration: Verify the supplier's legal registration status and business scope to ensure they are a legitimate entity capable of producing the goods. This can be done through the official business registry for the country of manufacture, such as China's National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (gsxt.gov.cn). 
1
Factory Operations: Confirm that the factory is using the materials specified and that their production setup matches what was quoted. This is crucial for cost and quality control. 
1
Quality Control: Ensure that the supplier has established proper quality control processes and has the necessary certifications to meet Australian standards. 
1
Supplier Compliance: Check for any compliance issues, such as safety regulations, environmental standards, and labor practices, to ensure the supplier operates within legal and ethical boundaries. 
1
Shipping and Logistics: Verify the supplier's shipping capabilities and logistics arrangements to ensure timely and secure delivery of goods to Australia. 
1

These checks are essential to protect the interests of the Australian importer and to ensure a successful and compliant import process.
