Unified Social Credit Code Explained: The 18-Digit China Business ID
The Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) is the primary identifier for every legal entity in China. It is an 18-character alphanumeric code that serves as the company's unique national identity number. This code replaced the previously separate business registration number, organization code, and tax ID. The USCC is issued once at the time of registration and never reused after dissolution, making it a crucial tool for verifying the legitimacy and authenticity of Chinese companies. 
chinverify.com

To verify a USCC, you can search for the company on China's official National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System (GSXT) at gsxt.gov.cn. The search will return the registered legal name, status, legal representative, and registered capital, which must match the company's business license exactly. This verification is essential for confirming the business license is real and for pulling litigation or penalty records.
