Warning Signs Suggesting False Business License or Certificates
Refuses to provide business license: A legitimate supplier should readily share their business license and registration details. 
1
Uses free email services: Suppliers using Gmail, Yahoo, or Hotmail instead of company domain email addresses may be hiding something or not an established business. 
1
Requests payment via Western Union or personal account: These payment methods offer zero buyer protection, making them dangerous. 
1
Refuses factory visit or video call: Legitimate factories are proud to show their facilities and want you to see their capabilities. 
1
Prices significantly below market average: Quotes that are 40% or more below average are suspicious and may indicate a false business license. 
1

These warning signs should prompt caution and further investigation when dealing with a Chinese supplier. Always verify the legitimacy of a supplier's business license and certificates to avoid potential scams and ensure a safe transaction.
