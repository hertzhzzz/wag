To verify that a Chinese supplier can legally export your product, you should request the following evidence:
Business License: A valid business license is a primary official document for any legal entity, including Chinese suppliers. It should be publicly searchable and match the supplier's claims. 
6
Unified Social Credit Code: This is an 18-character alphanumeric identifier that functions like a corporate ID number. It should appear on the business license and match what you find online. 
6
Export License: If the supplier is involved in export transactions, they should have their own export license or disclose the export agent used. 
6
Government Registry: Check the supplier's registration status, including establishment date, registered capital, legal representative, shareholders, business scope, and any administrative penalties or abnormal operation flags. 
6
Credit History: Verify the supplier's credit history to ensure they are financially stable and can meet their obligations. 
6

These checks will help you confirm that the supplier is legally authorized to export your product and that they have the necessary documentation to comply with export regulations.
