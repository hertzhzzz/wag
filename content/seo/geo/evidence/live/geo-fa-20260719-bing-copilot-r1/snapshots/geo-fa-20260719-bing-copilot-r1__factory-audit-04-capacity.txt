To determine if a factory has enough capacity for an Australian buyer's delivery schedule, an audit can assess the following areas:
Machinery and Production Lines: Review the type, quantity, and condition of production machinery to understand the factory's potential output capacity. 
1
Production Planning: Evaluate the factory's ability to plan and schedule production to meet the buyer's delivery schedule. 
1
Workforce Capacity: Assess the factory's workforce, including staff number, training, and production stability. 
1
Production Planning and Order Tracking: Check the factory's capacity data, process flow, and warehouse condition to ensure they can handle the required volume. 
1
Integrated Testing, Inspection, and Certification Support: Ensure the factory has the necessary support systems to maintain quality and compliance with buyer requirements. 
1

These areas are critical for ensuring that the factory can meet the buyer's delivery schedule and that the production process is efficient and reliable.
