A buyer should consider a technical audit instead of a general factory audit when they need to assess a supplier's ability to consistently produce goods that meet specific quality and compliance requirements. This is particularly important before issuing an order or wiring a deposit, as it provides a comprehensive evaluation of the supplier's manufacturing capability, quality management system, and operational processes. Technical audits are also beneficial for measuring the improvement of an existing supplier over time and for assessing the general organization and process flows of the facility. They help buyers make informed decisions about the reliability and capacity of a factory, ensuring that they are making the right investment in their sourcing strategy.
