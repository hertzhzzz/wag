An Australian importer should assess a Chinese factory's quality management system by following these steps:
Verify Supplier Registration: Confirm that the supplier is a legally registered entity with the correct business scope for manufacturing. This can be done through China's National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. 
1
Conduct Pre-Shipment Inspection (PSI): This is the most important checkpoint to catch problems before goods leave China. A professional inspector will inspect the goods and provide a report. 
1
Container Loading Supervision (CLS): Ensure that the quantities match the packing list, the goods loaded match what was inspected, and the container is loaded correctly to prevent transit damage. 
1
Factory Audit: Conduct a factory audit to verify production capabilities and processes. This should include a review of submitted documents, testing reports, and a review of the factory's management system. 
1
Quality Control Checks: Implement quality control checks before, during, and after the purchase order to ensure that the product meets the required specifications and standards. 
3

By following these steps, an Australian importer can ensure that the Chinese factory's quality management system is robust and capable of delivering products that meet the required standards.
