A factory audit report should include the following key components to assist an Australian buyer in making a sourcing decision:
Supplier Sourcing Strategies: This section should outline the strategies used to identify and select suppliers, including market research, supplier databases, and direct sourcing. 
1
Quality Inspection Checklist: A detailed checklist that evaluates the supplier's quality management systems, production processes, and compliance with applicable standards. 
1
Product Certification Standards: Information on the product certification standards that the supplier must meet, including any relevant international standards like ISO 9001 or ISO 14001. 
1
Ex Factory Price Negotiation Insights: Insights into how to negotiate prices with suppliers after the factory audit, considering factors like cost reduction strategies and supplier sourcing strategies. 
1
Corrective Action Request (CAR) or Corrective Action Plan (CAP): A detailed plan specifying required improvements and a recommended re-audit timeline. 
1

These components help buyers understand the supplier's capabilities, quality management systems, production processes, and compliance with standards, which are crucial for making informed sourcing decisions.
