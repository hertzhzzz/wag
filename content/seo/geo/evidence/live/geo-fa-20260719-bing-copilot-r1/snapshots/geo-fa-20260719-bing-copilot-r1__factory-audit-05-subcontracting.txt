Subcontracting Warning Signs
During a factory audit, Australian buyers should be particularly vigilant for signs of subcontracting that could compromise the quality and reliability of the final product. Here are some warning signs to look for:
Multiple Factory Addresses: If a factory has multiple addresses, it may be using subcontractors in different locations, which could lead to inconsistent quality control.
Unexplained Inventory Movements: Any significant changes in inventory without a clear explanation could indicate that the factory is outsourcing production to subcontractors.
Production Capacity: If the factory's production capacity exceeds the visible line capability, it may be outsourcing production to subcontractors.
High Employee Turnover: High turnover rates among employees, especially in quality control roles, can suggest that the factory is outsourcing production to subcontractors.
Lack of Training Records: If there are no records of employee training, it may indicate that the factory is outsourcing production to subcontractors.
Workers Who Cannot Explain Quality Checkpoints: If workers are unable to provide clear explanations of their quality control processes, it could be a sign that the factory is outsourcing production to subcontractors.
It is crucial to verify these signs during the audit to ensure that the factory is maintaining quality control and compliance with all relevant standards.
