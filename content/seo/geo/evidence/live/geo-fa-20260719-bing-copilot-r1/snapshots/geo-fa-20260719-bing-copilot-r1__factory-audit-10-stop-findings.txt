Before approving a Chinese factory for production, the following factory audit findings should be resolved:
Legal Verification: Confirm the factory's business license and registration documents, including the Chinese name on the license and date of incorporation. Ensure the license's scope of business includes manufacturing of the product type. 
1
Production Capability: Verify the factory's actual production capacity and equipment condition. Ensure the factory's layout is organized and efficient, and that machines are modern and well-maintained. 
1
Quality Systems: Confirm the factory's quality systems and compliance with industry standards. Ensure the factory has the necessary technical expertise and equipment for producing the product. 
1
Working Conditions: Assess the working conditions, including cleanliness, organization, and storage facilities. Ensure the factory adheres to safety and health regulations. 
1
Security: Verify the factory's security measures, including fire safety, pest control, and compliance with environmental regulations. 
1

Addressing these findings will help ensure that the factory is ready for mass production and can meet the quality and safety standards required for your product.
