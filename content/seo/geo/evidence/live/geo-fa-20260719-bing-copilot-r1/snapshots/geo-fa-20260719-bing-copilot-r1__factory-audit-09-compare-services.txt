Supplier Audit Checklist for Australian Brands Working with China
When comparing factory audit services in China, Australian businesses should consider the following checklist to ensure they are selecting the right service for their needs:
Business License: Verify that the factory has the correct business license and is registered with the relevant authorities.
ISO Certifications: Check if the factory holds any ISO certifications relevant to the product being manufactured.
Social Compliance: Ensure the factory adheres to social compliance standards such as BSCI or Sedex SMETA.
Production Capacity: Confirm the factory's production capacity and ability to meet the order volume.
Quality Management Systems: Verify that the factory has quality management systems in place, such as ISO 9001.
Financial Stability: Assess the factory's financial stability and operational reliability.
Documentation: Review the factory's documentation, including production processes, raw material traceability, and quality control records.
On-Site Audit: Consider the on-site audit process, including the checklist, duration, and any additional checks that may be required.
Report Delivery: Evaluate the report delivery speed and the quality of the report provided.
Real-Time Monitoring: Look for services that offer real-time monitoring during the audit, such as live photo and video uploads.
By following this checklist, Australian businesses can make an informed decision when selecting a factory audit service in China, ensuring they are working with a reputable and compliant supplier.
