An auditor reviewing a Chinese factory should examine the following documents and production records to ensure compliance with industry standards and regulatory requirements:
Business License: Verify the factory's legitimacy and business scope.
Production Records: Review the factory's production history and documentation.
Quality Control Records: Check the factory's quality control processes and documentation.
Safety Protocols: Assess the factory's safety measures and documentation.
Social Compliance Records: Verify adherence to labor standards and ethical practices.
Certifications and Certificates: Confirm the factory's adherence to industry-specific certifications and standards.
Labor Records: Review the factory's labor practices and employee records.
Compliance Documentation: Ensure the factory maintains documentation that complies with local and international regulations.
These documents and records are essential for an auditor to assess the factory's operational efficiency, safety, and adherence to quality standards, which are crucial for the production of safe and ethical products.
