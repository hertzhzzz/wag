## Sports Merchandise Licensing and China Manufacturing

The Premier League merchandise supply chain reveals how global sports brands engage Chinese manufacturing for Australian consumers.

Licensed Premier League merchandise production involves multiple tiers: brand owners (clubs and official licensees), manufacturers (primarily in China, Vietnam, and Indonesia), and distribution networks. Australian importers typically interact at the distribution level or as downstream retailers purchasing from authorised distributors.

The KAETA tariff elimination on UK-origin merchandise has shifted competitive dynamics for parallel importers. Official licensed merchandise now competes more effectively against grey market imports on price, since preferential tariff treatment reduces the cost advantage that parallel importers previously held through tariff avoidance.

For Australian businesses sourcing Premier League merchandise directly from manufacturers, the key considerations are licensing legitimacy, product quality consistency, and supply chain traceability. Unofficial merchandise production in China continues despite enforcement actions, and Australian customs has increased scrutiny of merchandise imports that may infringe club trademarks.
