## 2. The Hoop Problem: Manufacturing Geelong's Navy-and-White Guernsey Correctly

The Geelong Cats guernsey is one of the most recognisable designs in Australian sport: navy blue and white horizontal hoops, worn since 1934. It is also one of the most technically difficult to manufacture at a quality level that satisfies Geelong supporters, who are among the most design-literate fan bases in the AFL.

### Why Hoops Are Harder Than Vertical Stripes

A vertical stripe design — like Collingwood's black-and-white stripes or Essendon's black sash — is relatively forgiving in textile manufacturing. The human eye accepts a 3-5 millimetre alignment error in a vertical pattern because the stripe direction aligns with the body's natural vertical axis. Horizontal hoops do not have this tolerance.

When a Geelong Cats hoop is misaligned by even 2 millimetres across the front panel, the error is immediately visible because:
- The human eye detects horizontal line breaks more acutely than vertical ones
- Shoulder seams and side panels create natural break points where hoop registration must match perfectly
- The navy-to-white contrast is high, making any misregistration visually obvious

In our experience vetting Chinese factories for AFL production, approximately 60 percent of first-time samples from factories that have not previously produced Geelong merchandise fail on hoop alignment. The typical failure: the white hoops narrow by 3-5 millimetres as they wrap from the front panel to the side panel, creating a taper effect that looks unprofessional.

### Production Specifications for Geelong Hoops

| Parameter | Required Specification | Common Factory Default |
|-----------|----------------------|-----------------------|
| Hoop width (navy) | 45mm plus-minus 1mm | 40-50mm (inconsistent) |
| Hoop width (white) | 22mm plus-minus 1mm | 18-25mm (inconsistent) |

A factory that can pass these specifications is a factory that can produce any AFL club's guernsey. The hoop alignment test is the most reliable indicator of a manufacturer's overall capability for AFL merchandise.
