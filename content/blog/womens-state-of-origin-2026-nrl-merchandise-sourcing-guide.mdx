## The Rise of Women's State of Origin

### A Rapidly Growing Property

Women's State of Origin has experienced remarkable growth since its establishment in 2018. Attendance figures have increased dramatically, with the 2025 match drawing record crowds that fill major stadiums. Television viewership has followed suit, with millions of Australians tuning in to watch the women's representative fixtures.

This growth reflects broader trends in women's sports globally, but the NRL Women's (NRLW) competition and the State of Origin showcase have accelerated faster than most comparable leagues worldwide. The 2026 Women's State of Origin is positioned to break the records set in previous years, with growing media coverage and corporate sponsorship investment.

### The Commercial Opportunity

Despite the rapid growth of women's rugby league, the merchandise market for women's NRL products has not kept pace. Many retailers continue to treat women's merchandise as a subset of the broader NRL market rather than a distinct category with its own consumer preferences, sizing requirements, and design aesthetics.

This gap creates a genuine blue ocean opportunity for Australian retailers. The women who attend State of Origin matches and follow the NRLW competition are passionate, engaged fans who want products designed for them—not simply smaller versions of men's jerseys. Retailers who recognise this distinction and source appropriate merchandise can capture a loyal customer base with significant growth potential.

## Understanding the Women's Rugby League Fan

### Who Is the Women's NRL Fan?

The audience for women's rugby league differs from the traditional NRL fan base in several important ways that affect merchandise preferences. The Women's State of Origin audience skews younger and includes a higher proportion of female attendees than men's fixtures. Family groups are more prominent, and the atmosphere at women's matches is often described as more family-friendly and inclusive.

In terms of purchasing behaviour, women's sports fans tend to prioritise quality and design over brand logos, prefer apparel that works both at the game and in daily life, value inclusive sizing (4XS through 4XL is increasingly expected), and respond to authenticity and connection with players rather than purely team loyalty.

## Sourcing from China for the Women's Sports Market

### Finding the Right Manufacturers

Chinese manufacturers with capabilities suited to women's sports merchandise exist across several manufacturing clusters. The key is identifying factories with experience in women's apparel cut and sew, understanding of Australian sizing standards, capability to produce women's-specific fits (not unisex or men's patterns), and experience with technical fabrics for performance wear.

Major manufacturing regions for women's apparel in China include:

Guangdong province, particularly Guangzhou and Dongguan, which hosts numerous factories with experience in women's fashion and sportswear. Zhejiang province, where manufacturers in and around Hangzhou produce higher-end women's apparel. Jiangsu province, which has capability in technical fabrics and performance sportswear.

### Specifications for Women's Sports Merchandise

When sourcing women's NRL merchandise from China, specifications matter more than for unisex products. Key considerations include:

Size grading that accounts for Australian women's body shapes, not simply scaled-down men's patterns. Fabric draping and stretch that provide comfortable fit across a range of body types. Neckline and sleeve treatments that suit women's preferences (crew necks, scoop necks, raglan sleeves). Length proportions that work for women of average to above-average Australian heights. Colour options that go beyond pink versions of team colours to include the full team palette.

Providing detailed specification sheets with reference garments or fit models significantly improves the accuracy of Chinese manufacturers' first samples and reduces the sampling iteration cycle.

## The NRL Licensing Landscape

### NRL Women's Licensing Structure

The NRL manages licensing for women's rugby league through the same central structure as the men's competition, with the NRL's commercial arm controlling trademarks for the league, all clubs, and representative teams including the State of Origin brands.

Licensed merchandise for women's NRL is produced by major sportswear partners including ISC and Classic Sportswear, who hold rights to produce team apparel including women's-specific jerseys and training wear. These licensees manufacture through their global supply chains, which include Chinese and Southeast Asian factories.

### Licensing Considerations for Women's Merchandise

The licensing environment for women's NRL merchandise presents both opportunities and challenges. On the positive side, the NRL has expressed strong interest in growing the women's merchandise market and has been more receptive to licensing proposals focused on women's products than some comparable sports organisations.

However, the existing licensing structure means that independent retailers seeking official women's NRL merchandise must either purchase from authorised wholesale distributors or negotiate their own licensing arrangements, which typically require minimum guarantees and category exclusivity that can be challenging for small to medium retailers.

### The Lifestyle Merchandise Opportunity

A particularly promising approach for independent retailers involves lifestyle-oriented merchandise that references women's rugby league without requiring formal NRL licensing. Examples include:

Original artwork celebrating women's rugby league players and culture. Apparel designed in team-inspired colourways without using team names or logos. Empowerment-themed merchandise referencing the rise of women's sport in Australia. Commemorative items marking the growth of the women's game.

This approach allows retailers to build brand identity around women's rugby league fandom while avoiding the costs and constraints of official licensing.

## Quality and Sizing Standards

### Getting Sizing Right

Sizing is arguably the most critical quality factor for women's sports merchandise. Australian women's sizing differs from both Asian and US sizing standards, and getting this wrong leads to high return rates and customer dissatisfaction.

When working with Chinese manufacturers on women's merchandise, Australian retailers should provide size specifications in Australian standard measurements, request samples across multiple sizes (not just the sample size), conduct fit testing with Australian women of different body types, and include size comparison charts showing the conversion between brand sizing and measurements.

Many Australian retailers who specialise in women's sportswear maintain their own block patterns and have Chinese manufacturers produce to their specifications rather than using the factory's standard blocks.

### Fabric Selection for Women's Merchandise

Fabric quality and hand feel are particularly important for women's sports merchandise. Women consumers are more likely to evaluate products based on tactile qualities and are less tolerant of stiff, low-quality fabrics than the general market.

Recommended fabric considerations include cotton-polyester blends with adequate stretch for comfortable movement, moisture-wicking performance fabrics for activewear and training items, brushed fleece for hoodies and track pants that is soft against the skin, and jersey knit for tees that offers good drape and breathability.

Requesting fabric swatches before production and testing for shrinkage, pilling, and colour fastness prevents quality surprises when the shipment arrives.

## FAQ

### What is the minimum order quantity for women's NRL merchandise from China?

MOQs vary by product category. Basic t-shirts and accessories typically start at 200-500 pieces per design. Performance apparel like women's jerseys requires higher minimums of 500-1000 pieces per design due to specialised fabric and construction requirements. Knitted accessories like scarves and beanies are available at 200-300 pieces per design.

### How do women's sizing requirements affect sourcing from China?

Chinese clothing manufacturers typically use Asian sizing standards, which run one to two sizes smaller than Australian equivalents for women's apparel. Australian retailers must provide precise size specifications and insist on samples that match Australian size expectations. Working with manufacturers experienced in the Australian market, or investing in a pattern-making service, eliminates most sizing issues.

### Can Australian retailers sell officially licensed women's NRL merchandise?

Yes, by purchasing from authorised wholesale distributors who hold NRL licences. This is the most straightforward path for most retailers. Independent licensing is possible but typically requires minimum guarantee commitments that may be challenging for smaller businesses.

### What product categories are most underserved in women's NRL merchandise?

Fashion-forward lifestyle apparel—including cropped tees, bomber jackets, leggings, and fitted hoodies designed specifically for women—remains significantly underserved. Most available merchandise is unisex sizing with generic styling. Accessories like tote bags, scrunchies, and jewellery in team colours are also underexplored categories.

### How can I test the women's NRL merchandise market without large inventory commitment?

Start with accessories like scarves and caps, which have lower MOQs and universal sizing. Use these to gauge demand for women's-specific products in your market. Consider print-on-demand or small-batch production for initial product testing. Gather customer feedback on sizing, style preferences, and price points before scaling to full apparel lines.

### What are the key differences between sourcing women's and unisex sports merchandise?

Women's merchandise requires more attention to fabric quality, fit and sizing, design aesthetics, and colour selection. The manufacturing complexity is higher because women's patterns are more varied and quality expectations are higher. However, the lack of competition in this segment means that retailers who get it right can achieve stronger margins and customer loyalty than in saturated unisex merchandise categories.

### How do I ensure Chinese manufacturers produce accurate women's sizing?

Provide detailed spec sheets with measurements in centimetres for every size point. Send reference garments that fit correctly in the Australian market. Require samples graded across multiple sizes, not just one sample size. Consider hiring an Australian-based pattern maker to create blocks that you provide to your manufacturer. Request photos of fit models wearing the samples for visual verification.

### What is the growth outlook for women's rugby league merchandise?

The outlook is strongly positive. Women's State of Origin and NRLW viewership and attendance continue to grow faster than any other segment of Australian sport. As more brands and retailers invest in women's-specific products, the market is expected to expand from its current estimated $40-50 million annually toward $100 million within the next 3-5 years. Early movers who establish supplier relationships and brand presence now will be well-positioned as the market matures.
