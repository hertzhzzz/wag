## Avoiding Common Pitfalls

### Intellectual Property Boundaries

National team crests and football federation logos are trademarked. You cannot legally reproduce the Royal Spanish Football Federation (RFEF) crest or the Iraq Football Association (IFA) logo without a licensing agreement.

**What you can legally produce:**
- Designs using national colours and flag motifs (these are public domain)
- Match-specific graphics and typography ("Spain vs Iraq 2026")
- Generic football imagery (balls, goal nets, stadium silhouettes)
- Original artwork that evokes national identity without copying protected marks

Chinese manufacturers are generally familiar with IP boundaries, but communicate your requirements explicitly. Have your designs reviewed before production begins. A single customs seizure of trademark-infringing goods can wipe out an entire production run's profit.

### Quality Control Must-Checks for Football Merchandise

Scarves and fabric merchandise from budget manufacturers can arrive with specific defects that ruin sellability. Order a pre-production sample and inspect for these before greenlighting bulk production:

- Colour bleeding between red/yellow (Spanish flag) and red/white/black (Iraqi flag) knitted sections
- Uneven knitting density causing visible thin patches
- Misaligned print registration on split-colour designs
- Arabic text rendered as scrambled characters (indicating the factory's RIP software lacks Arabic font support)
- Polyester flags with poor hem stitching that fray after minimal handling

A third-party inspection before shipment costs approximately $150-300 and is the cheapest insurance you will ever buy.

### Tariff and Customs Awareness

As of June 2026, textile imports into Australia attract a 5% customs duty under the applicable tariff classification. China-Australia Free Trade Agreement (ChAFTA) preferences may reduce or eliminate this duty if your supplier provides a valid Certificate of Origin. Always request this document -- it takes your supplier 15 minutes to prepare and can save you hundreds of dollars per shipment.

### Is it legal to sell football merchandise without an official license?

Yes, provided you do not reproduce trademarked logos, crests, wordmarks, or player likenesses without permission. Generic match-day merchandise that references the fixture using national colours, flag motifs, and non-trademarked descriptive text ("Spain vs Iraq 2026") is legal. Many successful merchandise businesses operate sustainably in this space without licensing agreements. The key is strict avoidance of protected intellectual property.

### What is the minimum order quantity for custom football scarves from China?

Most Yiwu-based jacquard-knit scarf manufacturers accept MOQs starting at 100 units. Simpler printed polyester scarves can go as low as 50 units.
