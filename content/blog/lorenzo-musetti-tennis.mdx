## How to Vet a Chinese Factory for Tennis Player Merchandise

**Request fabric swatches in the specific colours your design requires.** Musetti's on-court aesthetic draws from Italian racing green, deep navy, and optic white — colours that look different under Australian sunlight than they do under factory lighting. Request physical swatches shipped to your Australian address before approving production. A factory that pushes back on swatch requests is a factory that does not trust its own colour consistency.

**Verify sublimation printing capability.** Player-inspired designs that reference signature shots or playing styles typically require full-colour sublimation printing rather than screen printing. Sublimation embeds dye into the fabric fibres rather than laying ink on top of them, producing a print that does not crack, peel, or fade through machine washing. Chinese factories with in-house sublimation capability produce better results than factories that outsource printing to third-party vendors.
