## 1. Executive Summary

Shenzhen Ezpro Acoustics & Lighting Technology Co., Ltd. (brand: Ezpro / EK, established as a limited company in 2013 and converted to a joint-stock company in 2018) is a professional audio-visual system integrator headquartered in Shenzhen's Nanshan District Science and Technology Park. The company provides end-to-end AV solutions encompassing design consulting, equipment supply, system integration, commissioning, and post-installation maintenance across professional audio, lighting, conference systems, and central control platforms.

EZPro operates as a system integrator rather than a manufacturer, with its core value proposition resting on engineering design capability, brand agency relationships with over 20 international AV brands, and large-scale project delivery track record. The company has filed for IPO on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange ChiNext board three times (all subsequently withdrawn, most recently in March 2023). 

**Key Strengths:** Registered capital of RMB 90 million with an IPO-calibre financial and compliance foundation; 21 product categories spanning the full AVLC (Audio, Video, Lighting, Control) spectrum; proven delivery on landmark projects including the State Council Information Office press briefing hall, Huawei global conference standardisation, and the Shenzhen World Exhibition & Convention Center; headquartered in Shenzhen Science and Technology Park with strong talent access.

**Areas of Note:** Three IPO withdrawals raise questions about capital market viability; core equipment relies on overseas-imported brands creating supply chain exposure; the Allen & Heath agency relationship was discontinued in 2025; as a system integrator rather than a manufacturer, the engagement model differs fundamentally from factory-direct procurement — buyers are purchasing engineering capability and supply chain orchestration rather than manufactured goods.

## 4. Production & Technical Capability

### 4.1 Service Delivery Model

EZPro operates a **system integration and engineering services model** rather than a conventional manufacturing operation. The company's core activities are:

| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Solution Design | AV system architecture design, acoustic modelling, system schematic development |
| Equipment Supply | Procurement and supply of branded equipment from international manufacturers (agency and distribution relationships) |
| System Integration | On-site installation, commissioning, calibration, and system tuning |
| Software Platform | In-house developed ezCloud management platform for AV system monitoring and control |
| After-Sales Service | Maintenance contracts, technical support, spare parts management |

**Headquarters and Facilities:**

The company is headquartered in Building E2, TCL Science Park, Nanshan District, Shenzhen. The facility serves primarily as a design, engineering, and project management centre rather than a manufacturing plant. For equipment supply, EZPro warehouses and distributes products from represented brands; product manufacturing is conducted by the respective brand principals (e.g., EAW in the United States, Powersoft in Italy, YAMAHA in Japan).

**Self-Developed Products:**

EZPro has developed proprietary product lines under its in-house brands:
- **ezacoustics** — Professional loudspeaker systems
- **ezCloud** — AV system management and monitoring software platform

These self-developed products represent the company's in-house engineering capability, while the majority of delivered systems incorporate equipment from the international brands the company represents.

### 4.2 Quality Control Framework

As a system integrator, EZPro's quality control operates at two levels:

**Equipment-Level Quality:** Relies on the quality assurance systems of each represented brand's manufacturing facilities. EZPro's role is to verify that supplied equipment meets project specifications and to manage warranty claims with brand principals.

**Integration-Level Quality:** EZPro's in-house quality assurance covers system design accuracy, installation workmanship, commissioning calibration, and system performance verification. The company's Grade I qualifications in audio, lighting, and AV system integration require demonstrated quality management processes as part of the qualification maintenance.

**Quality Observations:**
- The company's track record on landmark projects (State Council, Huawei, Shenzhen World) suggests established quality management practices for large-scale deployments
- As a non-manufacturer, EZPro's quality control scope is different from a factory — the key quality risk lies in integration design and commissioning accuracy rather than manufacturing defects
- Buyers should clarify the warranty structure: what is covered by EZPro directly versus what flows through to individual brand manufacturers

## Appendix: Terms Glossary

[^1]: **Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码):** An 18-digit identifier assigned to every legal entity registered in China, consolidating the business licence, organisation code, and tax registration into a single number. Verifiable through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System at https://www.gsxt.gov.cn.

[^2]: **Legal Representative (法定代表人):** The individual designated under Chinese company law who exercises the powers of the company and assumes corresponding legal responsibilities. In the Chinese corporate system, the Legal Representative bears personal accountability for regulatory compliance and can face personal travel or business restrictions if the company breaches laws.

[^3]: **Registered Capital (注册资本):** The total capital amount declared at company incorporation. Under China's post-2014 company law, this is a subscribed figure rather than fully paid capital. The RMB 90 million registered capital for EZPro indicates substantial declared shareholder commitment.

[^4]: **Administration for Market Regulation (市场监督管理局):** The local government body responsible for business registration, market supervision, and consumer protection. Each city/district has its own AMR office with jurisdiction over companies registered at that address. EZPro is registered under the Shenzhen Nanshan AMR.

[^5]: **ChiNext (Growth Enterprise Market / 创业板):** A NASDAQ-style board of the Shenzhen Stock Exchange established in 2009 for high-growth, innovative enterprises. ChiNext listing requires comprehensive financial auditing, regulatory disclosure, and sponsor (investment bank) due diligence. EZPro's three ChiNext IPO filings (all withdrawn) indicate the company has undergone multiple rounds of institutional due diligence, though the withdrawals reflect challenges in completing the listing process.
