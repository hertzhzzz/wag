## 1. Executive Summary

Shenzhen Haijixing Technology Co., Ltd. (established 2013) is a specialised OEM/ODM manufacturer of digital video cameras, action cameras, and body cameras headquartered in Shenzhen's Guangming District. The company operates a 1,660 sqm assembly facility with 108 employees and 49 production and testing equipment units.

Haijixing holds SGS facility certification and maintains FCC certification (Grantee Code 2A6Q7) covering over 20 product models from 1080P to 6K resolution, with continuous FCC filings from 2022 to 2025. The company serves export markets including Turkey, the United Kingdom, Canada, the United States, India, and Australia, operating primarily through OEM/ODM arrangements where products are sold under buyer labels.

**Key Strengths:** Established 13-year operational history, comprehensive FCC compliance across product range, SGS-certified facility, demonstrated product development from 1080P to 6K over three years.
**Areas of Note:** Assembly-only manufacturing model with core components externally sourced, modest registered capital of RMB 2 million, limited warehouse capacity of 430 sqm.

## Appendix: Terms Glossary

[^1]: **Legal Representative (法定代表人):** Under Chinese corporate law, a specific individual designated as the company's legal representative who bears personal legal liability for the company's actions.

[^2]: **Registered Capital (注册资本):** The total capital amount declared at company incorporation. Under China's post-2014 company law, this is a subscribed figure rather than fully paid capital.

[^3]: **Administration for Market Regulation (市场监督管理局):** The local government body responsible for business registration, market supervision, and consumer protection.

[^4]: **CE (European Conformity):** A mandatory conformity marking for products sold within the European Economic Area, indicating compliance with EU health, safety, and environmental standards.
