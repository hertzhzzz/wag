## 1. Executive Summary

Guangzhou Ceopa Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. (brand: CEOPA / Xipai), operating as a joint-stock limited company registered in April 2017, presents itself as a manufacturer and system integrator of public broadcast, conference, and professional audio equipment. The company is headquartered in Guangzhou's Panyu District, within the Pearl River Delta's professional audio-visual manufacturing cluster.

This supplier carries a **confirmed_with_conflicts** assessment status due to significant discrepancies between marketing claims and independently verifiable corporate records. The most material finding is an entity-level mismatch: the ICP filing licence for the company's online domains is registered to a different legal entity — Guangzhou Laiao Optoelectronics Technology Co., Ltd. (established 2010) — rather than the current operating entity. Additionally, the brand narrative of "German origin" and 20-plus years of history does not align with the legal entity's 2017 incorporation date.

**Key Strengths:** Broad product portfolio across public broadcast, conference systems, and professional audio; DAV Digital Audio-Visual Engineering "Top 10 Public Broadcast Solutions Brand" recognition; claimed in-house manufacturing capability via the "CEOPA Industrial Park" facility.

**Areas of Note (Critical):** ICP licence entity mismatch — the domain legal registrant is a separate company; brand history claims (20+ years, German origin) are unsupported by corporate registration records; a predecessor limited company has been deregistered; the official website (ceopa.com) returns HTTP 403; multiple material claims — certifications, factory infrastructure, international project references — remain unverified; registered capital of RMB 10 million is modest for a manufacturing entity claiming full in-house production.

## 2. Business Registration & Corporate Information

### ### 2.2 Entity Structure & ICP Filing Discrepancy

The corporate structure surrounding CEOPA involves multiple entities, creating a complex and potentially misleading picture for international buyers:

**Historical Entity — Deregistered:**
- Guangzhou Ceopa Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. (广州市西派电子科技有限公司) — a limited company (有限公司, not the current 股份有限公司) with legal representative Xu Junwu (徐君武), registered capital approximately RMB 3 million, incorporated approximately 2003. This entity has been **deregistered (注销)**. Marketing materials continue to reference its history.

**Related Entity:**
- Guangzhou Panyu Xipai Electronic Electrical Appliance Factory (广州市番禺区西派电子电器厂) — a general partnership with overlapping product lines, operating a separate website (las-pa.com).

**ICP Filing Discrepancy (Critical Finding):**
The ICP filing licence[^5] (粤ICP备2021091166号), which is the mandatory government registration required to operate a website in mainland China, is registered to **Guangzhou Laiao Optoelectronics Technology Co., Ltd. (广州莱澳光电科技有限公司, established 2010)** — a different legal entity from the current operating company. This means the legal registrant of the CEOPA web domains (ceopa.com.cn, ceopa.com) is not the company presenting itself as the manufacturer and contracting counterparty. This entity-layer separation is a significant compliance concern for international procurement[^6].

**Brand Timeline:**
- CEOPA brand domain (ceopa.com) registered in 2002 — 15 years before the current legal entity was incorporated
- Current joint-stock company incorporated: April 2017
- Brand marketing claims "German origin" and 20-plus years of operational history
- Official website ceopa.com currently returns HTTP 403 Forbidden (nginx anti-crawl configuration); brand product catalogue is primarily accessible through the third-party DAV platform (ceopa.corp.dav01.com)

**Operational Context:**
The company is located in Guangzhou's Panyu District, part of the Pearl River Delta professional audio-visual equipment manufacturing cluster that hosts approximately 600 enterprises in the entertainment technology sector. ## Appendix: Terms Glossary

[^1]: **Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码):** An 18-digit identifier assigned to every legal entity registered in China, consolidating the business licence, organisation code, and tax registration into a single number. Verifiable through the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System at https://www.gsxt.gov.cn.

[^2]: **Legal Representative (法定代表人):** Under Chinese corporate law, a specific individual designated as the company's legal representative who bears personal legal liability for the company's actions. This role is distinct from a company director in Australian law — the Legal Representative is personally accountable for regulatory violations and can face travel restrictions or personal penalties.

[^3]: **Registered Capital (注册资本):** The total capital amount declared by shareholders at company incorporation. Under China's post-2014 company law, this is a subscribed figure — shareholders commit to contributing this amount but actual payment (paid-in capital) may occur over time. The ratio of paid-in to registered capital indicates fulfilment of shareholder commitments.

[^4]: **Administration for Market Regulation (市场监督管理局):** The local government body responsible for business registration, market supervision, and consumer protection. Each city/district has its own AMR office with jurisdiction over companies registered within its territory.

[^5]: **ICP Filing Licence (ICP备案):** A mandatory government registration required under Chinese law to operate any website hosted on servers within mainland China. The ICP licence number appears in the website footer and identifies the legal entity responsible for the website's content. Operating a website without a valid ICP filing, or with a filing registered to a different entity, is a regulatory violation. The ICP number for CEOPA domains is 粤ICP备2021091166号.

[^6]: **Entity Discrepancy — ICP Registrant vs. Operating Entity:** The ICP filing licence for ceopa.com and ceopa.com.cn is registered to Guangzhou Laiao Optoelectronics Technology Co., Ltd. (广州莱澳光电科技有限公司, established 2010), not to the current operating entity Guangzhou Ceopa Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. (广州市西派电子科技股份有限公司, established 2017). This means the legal entity responsible for the website content under Chinese internet regulations is different from the entity presenting itself as the manufacturer and prospective contracting party. For international procurement, this entity-layer separation creates material uncertainty about legal accountability, contractual counterparty identification, and payment entity verification. This finding was produced through independent investigation supplementary to standard corporate registry searches.

[^7]: **CCC (China Compulsory Certification / 3C认证):** Mandatory safety certification required for products sold in the Chinese market, covering electrical safety and electromagnetic compatibility. While CCC is a China-specific requirement and does not substitute for Australian RCM, successful CCC testing demonstrates products have passed rigorous safety evaluation.

[^8]: **CE Marking (Conformite Europeenne):** Indicates conformity with health, safety, and environmental protection standards for products sold within the European Economic Area. While not directly valid in Australia, CE testing data is often accepted as a reference by NATA-accredited laboratories when assessing products for RCM certification.

[^9]: **National High-Tech Enterprise (国家高新技术企业):** A government designation issued jointly by the Ministry of Science and Technology, Ministry of Finance, and State Taxation Administration. Companies must meet thresholds for R&D investment as a percentage of revenue, proportion of technical staff, and intellectual property ownership. The designation confers a reduced corporate income tax rate (15% versus the standard 25%).

[^10]: **SRDI "Little Giant" (专精特新"小巨人"):** A national-level government designation for SMEs demonstrating Specialization, Refinement, Differentiation, and Innovation in their field. Part of China's industrial policy to cultivate niche industry leaders. This designation has not been identified for the current CEOPA legal entity.
