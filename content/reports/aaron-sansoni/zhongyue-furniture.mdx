## 1. Executive Summary

Xianghe Zhongyue Bohua Metal Products Co., Ltd. (established 2018) is a manufacturer of integrated monitoring consoles, computer workstations, lecterns, and technical furniture headquartered in Xianghe County, Hebei Province. The company operates a 10,000 sqm facility with 66 employees, holds CE certification, and has registered seven product patents.

Zhongyue Bohua is recognised as a Technology-Based SME and A-Level Taxpayer. The company's product portfolio of 110 items focuses on control room furniture, surveillance monitoring consoles, and technical workstation solutions — categories directly applicable to broadcast studio, control room, and production environment requirements.

**Key Strengths:** CE-certified products for European market access, seven patents indicating in-house design capability, 10,000 sqm production facility, Technology-Based SME designation.
**Areas of Note:** Located in Hebei Province (outside the primary Guangdong manufacturing clusters), relatively young company (2018), limited publicly available information on specific broadcast-industry client references.
