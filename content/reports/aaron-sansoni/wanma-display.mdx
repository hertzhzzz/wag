## 1. Executive Summary

Shenzhen Wanma Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. (brand: W-Media, established 2005) is a manufacturer of LCD-based display products headquartered in Shenzhen, Guangdong Province. The company operates a 3,500 sqm facility with 40 employees and offers over 500 products across touch displays, digital signage, advertising kiosks, interactive self-service terminals, and educational display systems.

Wanma has approximately 20 years of operational history in the LCD display manufacturing sector. The company serves clients requiring customised display solutions including touch-enabled interactive panels, wall-mounted advertising displays, and self-service terminal hardware. Its products are applicable to commercial display, retail signage, educational technology, and studio monitoring environments.

**Key Strengths:** Two decades of operational history, broad product portfolio spanning 500+ display products, in-house R&D and customisation capability.
**Areas of Note:** Small workforce for a company of this age (40 employees), modest production scale relative to the display manufacturing industry.
