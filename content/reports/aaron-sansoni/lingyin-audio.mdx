## 1. Executive Summary

Guangzhou Lingyin Electronic Equipment Co., Ltd. (established 2005) is a manufacturer of public broadcast systems, professional loudspeakers, and microphones headquartered in Guangzhou's Baiyun District, Guangdong Province. The company operates a 12,000 sqm facility with 100 employees and is recognised as a National High-Tech Enterprise and A-Level Taxpayer.

With 21 years of operational history, Lingyin has developed a product portfolio of 144 items spanning public address systems, installed sound reinforcement, and professional audio equipment. The company's location in Guangzhou's Baiyun District places it within the Pearl River Delta professional audio manufacturing cluster.

**Key Strengths:** Two decades of industry experience, substantial factory footprint (12,000 sqm), national high-tech enterprise designation, broad product portfolio across public address and professional audio.
**Areas of Note:** Limited publicly available certification detail beyond national designations, product portfolio breadth suggests assembly and integration capabilities rather than core component manufacturing.
