## 1. Executive Summary

Guangzhou Lingyin Electronic Equipment Co., Ltd. (established 2017) is a manufacturer of public broadcast systems, professional loudspeakers, and microphones headquartered in Guangzhou's Baiyun District, Guangdong Province. The company operates a 12,000 sqm facility with a registered capital of RMB 140.45 million and is recognised as a National High-Tech Enterprise.

With 9 years of operational history, Lingyin has developed a product portfolio of 144 items spanning public address systems, installed sound reinforcement, and professional audio equipment. Its location in Baiyun District places it within the Pearl River Delta professional audio manufacturing cluster, which produces a significant share of the world's audio equipment.

**Key Strengths:** 9 years of industry experience, substantial 12,000 sqm factory footprint, National High-Tech Enterprise designation, broad product portfolio across public address and professional audio.
**Areas of Note:** Product-level certifications beyond national designations should be verified for specific export markets, product portfolio breadth suggests assembly and integration capabilities.
