## 1. Executive Summary

Guangdong Bolang Lighting & Audio Technology Co., Ltd. (brand: **Color** / 博朗; formerly Guangzhou Wolang Lighting Equipment Co., Ltd.) is a Guangzhou-based professional entertainment lighting manufacturer established in 2010. The company operates under the English brand name "Color Imagination" and is headquartered in the Nansha District of Guangzhou.

Bolang holds National High-Tech Enterprise status (verified through official government records, 2019 renewal) and a portfolio of 131 registered patents. Its product range covers over 237 active SKUs across moving heads (beam, wash, spot, 3-in-1, effect), film and television studio lights (Fresnel, profile, panel, CYC), PAR/LED fixtures, and strobe/blinder units — making it one of the more product-breadth-complete mid-tier stage lighting manufacturers in the Guangzhou cluster.

**Key Strengths:** Verified High-Tech Enterprise status; broad product catalogue directly including film/TV and broadcast studio fixtures; multiple international certifications (CE, FCC, 3C); factory-direct pricing with 500+ documented project cases; active international trade show presence.

**Areas of Note:** Company rebranded from "Wolang" to "Bolang/Color" ca. 2019; overall scale sits below top-tier domestic players.

## 4. Production & Technical Capability

### 4.1 Manufacturing Infrastructure

Bolang operates a dedicated manufacturing facility in the Nansha District of Guangzhou, within the Dihuaao Science and Technology Industrial Park. The facility supports the full product lifecycle from R&D to in-house production. Core manufacturing capabilities include:

- LED driver and control electronics assembly
- Moving head mechanism fabrication and integration
- Optical assembly (lens, reflector, gobo wheel systems)
- Automated aging and burn-in testing lines
- In-house firmware flashing and QC verification

The 2014 construction of an in-house showroom and the sequential addition of dedicated business units (cultural engineering 2018; market/R&D/marketing 2019) indicate measured capacity growth over the past decade.

### 4.2 R&D Capability

Bolang operates a dedicated R&D team focused on LED source control, motion mechanism design, and embedded firmware. The company describes its operations as integrating software development, manufacturing, sales, and service.
