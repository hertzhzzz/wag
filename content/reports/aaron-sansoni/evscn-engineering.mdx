## 1. Executive Summary

Guangzhou Evscn Electronics Co., Ltd. (brand: EVSCN / Yiwei, established 2003) is a comprehensive intelligent systems integrator headquartered in Guangzhou Science City within the Guangzhou High-Tech Industrial Development Zone. The company's core business spans professional audio-visual systems, stage lighting/sound/mechanical engineering, building intelligence, emergency command systems, intelligent conferencing, acoustic fit-out, floodlighting, and software development.

The company's legal representative, Deng Weidong (邓伟东), exercises highly concentrated control across nearly all surviving subsidiaries. Registered capital stands at RMB 50.09 million, positioning the company in the mid-to-upper tier among Chinese systems integrators.

As a systems integrator rather than a manufacturer, EVSCN's value proposition lies in its multi-disciplinary design-to-delivery capability spanning AV, stage engineering, building intelligence, and emergency command -- offering turnkey project delivery across sectors that would otherwise require coordination of multiple specialist contractors.

**Key Strengths:** Established operational history (2003), substantial registered capital (RMB 50M+), comprehensive multi-disciplinary service coverage enabling single-contractor project delivery, location within a national-level high-tech development zone.
**Areas of Note:** Limited independently verifiable certifications (Pending supplier provision), no identified export track record.

### 7.3 Contextual Notes

**Systems Integrator Risk Profile vs. Manufacturer Risk Profile:** Due diligence on a systems integrator differs from due diligence on a manufacturer. For a manufacturer, key risks centre on production consistency, material quality, and supply chain continuity. For an integrator, key risks centre on project management capability, engineering design competence, subcontractor management, and financial capacity to bond and deliver projects. The due diligence framework applied in this report reflects this distinction.

In commercial negotiations, it is worth confirming that Deng Weidong has authority to bind the contracting entity and that delegation of authority to project-level personnel is clearly documented.

## Appendix: Terms Glossary

[^1]: **Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码):** An 18-digit identifier assigned to every legal entity registered in China, consolidating the business licence, organisation code, and tax registration into a single number. Verifiable at https://www.gsxt.gov.cn.

[^2]: **Legal Representative (法定代表人):** The individual designated under Chinese company law who exercises the powers of the company and assumes corresponding legal responsibilities. This role carries personal legal accountability for company actions and is distinct from a company director under Australian law.

[^3]: **Registered Capital (注册资本):** The total capital amount declared at company incorporation. Under China's post-2014 company law, this is a subscribed figure rather than fully paid capital. It serves as an indicator of shareholder commitment rather than a measure of current financial capacity.

[^4]: **Administration for Market Regulation (市场监督管理局):** The local government body responsible for business registration, market supervision, and consumer protection. Each city/district has its own AMR office with jurisdiction over companies registered at that address.

[^5]: **Building Intelligence Engineering Professional Contracting Qualification (建筑智能化工程专业承包资质):** A tiered qualification system for construction and engineering contractors in China, issued by the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development. The qualification grade (Grade I, II, or III) determines the scale and complexity of projects a company is legally permitted to undertake. Verification of the specific grade held is recommended during commercial engagement.
