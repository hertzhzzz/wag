## 1. Executive Summary

Enping Yushi Electronic Technology Co., Ltd. (established 2019) is a specialised manufacturer of USB microphones, live-streaming microphones, and gaming microphones headquartered in Enping, Jiangmen, Guangdong Province. The company operates a 1,576 sqm facility with 40 employees and holds six design patents related to microphone products.

Enping is nationally recognised as China's primary microphone manufacturing cluster, accounting for approximately 60% of the country's microphone production. Yushi Electronics operates within this established industrial ecosystem, with a product focus on the USB digital microphone segment serving the live-streaming, content creation, and gaming markets.

**Key Strengths:** Located in China's dominant microphone manufacturing cluster, focused product portfolio in the growing USB/live-streaming segment, six design patents indicating in-house product development capability.
**Areas of Note:** Relatively young company (2019), small production workforce (40 employees), modest annual turnover (RMB 5-10 million range), limited certification portfolio beyond design patents.

## 6. Market Position & Industry Context

### 6.1 Enping: China's Microphone Capital

Enping, Jiangmen is China's largest microphone manufacturing cluster. The city hosts hundreds of electroacoustic enterprises producing microphones, audio mixers, amplifiers, and related accessories. Industrial output from microphone and audio equipment manufacturing in Enping serves both domestic and export markets, with products exported to over 120 countries.

Key advantages of sourcing from Enping include:
- **Supply chain density:** Microphone capsules, grilles, stands, pop filters, and USB audio chips all available within the city
- **Technical workforce:** Multi-generational workforce with microphone-specific manufacturing skills
- **Export infrastructure:** Established export logistics serving global markets
- **Cost competitiveness:** Cluster effects produce pricing advantages compared to dispersed manufacturing locations

### 6.2 USB Microphone Market Context

The USB microphone market has grown significantly with the expansion of live-streaming, podcasting, remote work, and content creation. USB microphones offer plug-and-play simplicity compared to traditional XLR microphones, making them the preferred format for non-professional studio setups. This market segment is price-sensitive and brand-diverse, creating opportunities for OEM/ODM manufacturers supplying third-party brands.
