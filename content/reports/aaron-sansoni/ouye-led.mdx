## 1. Executive Summary

Guangzhou Ouye Electronics Co., Ltd. (brand: Bozhihong, established 2014) is a manufacturer of indoor LED display screens headquartered in Guangzhou's Panyu District, Guangdong Province. The company operates a 1,080 sqm facility with 22 employees and a registered capital of RMB 5 million.

Ouye positions itself as a mid-scale LED screen manufacturer serving the Guangzhou region. The company provides R&D, production, sales, installation, and after-sales service as an integrated offering, with a product portfolio of 43 LED display products focused on indoor commercial applications.

**Key Strengths:** Located in Guangzhou's Panyu District within the Pearl River Delta electronics manufacturing cluster, registered capital of RMB 5 million indicating moderate financial substance, integrated service model from production through installation.
**Areas of Note:** Small workforce (22 employees), limited product range (43 products), modest production scale.

### 4.1 Manufacturing Model

Ouye operates as an LED display assembly and integration manufacturer. Key components — LED modules, driver ICs, power supplies, control cards, and metal enclosures — are sourced from the Pearl River Delta supply chain. The factory performs module assembly, cabinet construction, system integration, calibration, and packaging.

### 4.2 Product Categories

- Indoor LED display screens (fixed installation)
- Commercial advertising LED displays
- Conference and meeting room LED displays
- Custom LED display solutions

The company provides installation and after-sales service as part of its integrated offering, which distinguishes it from pure manufacturing-only operations.

## 5. Market Position

Ouye operates in the competitive mid-market segment of China's LED display manufacturing industry. Guangzhou's Panyu District is a major manufacturing hub for professional audio-visual and entertainment technology equipment, hosting an estimated 600 enterprises in the sector. ## Appendix: Terms Glossary

[^1]: **Registered Capital (注册资本):** The total capital amount declared at company incorporation. Under China's post-2014 company law, this is a subscribed figure rather than fully paid capital.

[^2]: **Administration for Market Regulation (市场监督管理局):** The local government body responsible for business registration, market supervision, and consumer protection.
