## ZONES

**Left Zone (Safe/Standard)**: Light background with navy accent. Shows the standard 30/70 payment split for first orders from Chinese suppliers. Key text: "30% Deposit" and "70% Balance Before Shipment". Simple factory silhouette icon.

**Right Zone (Red Flags)**: Warning amber background with darker amber border. Shows three red flags: "50%+ Deposit", "100% Upfront", "Personal Account Payment". Each flag clearly marked with warning icon.

**Center**: Subtle dividing line with "?" or balance scale icon between the two sides.
