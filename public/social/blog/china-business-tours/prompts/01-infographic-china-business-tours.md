# Cover Image Prompt — China Business Tours

## Subject
Professional B2B infographic cover for a comprehensive guide to China business tours for Australian companies. The image must communicate scale, professionalism, and the multi-city nature of a sourcing tour.

## Visual Zones (left to right flow)

**Zone 1 — Australian Buyer Arrival (left, 25%)**
- Business person silhouette with Australian flag accent
- Passport/travel documents icon
- Airport arrival visual cue

**Zone 2 — Multi-City Manufacturing Hub (center, 50%)**
- Bold title area: "CHINA BUSINESS TOUR" with year 2026
- Three factory city cards: Guangzhou | Shenzhen | Dongguan
- Each card: factory silhouette + city name + key stat
  - Guangzhou: "Canton Fair Hub / 150,000+ Exhibitors"
  - Shenzhen: "Electronics & Tech / 200+ Factories Visited"
  - Dongguan: "Manufacturing Center / 3-Day Avg Visit"
- Connecting dotted line between cities representing the tour route

**Zone 3 — Outcome (right, 25%)**
- Australian business success visual: handshake or checked-box
- "200+ Tours Completed" as prominent stat badge
- "5-7 Day Immersive Experience" duration callout

## Color Zones
- Navy (#0F2D5E): Zone 1 background, title bar, metric badges
- Amber (#F59E0B): Zone 2 accents, city card borders, stat highlights
- White: Zone backgrounds, text contrast areas

## Typography
- Bold sans-serif title: "CHINA BUSINESS TOUR 2026"
- Body: clean geometric letterforms
- Numbers: bold, oversized for key stats

## Composition Notes
- No gradients, no 3D effects, no shadows
- Clean flat vector shapes
- 16:9 horizontal layout
- Professional B2B aesthetic appropriate for Australian SME importers
