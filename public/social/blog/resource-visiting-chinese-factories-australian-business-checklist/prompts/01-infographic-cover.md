# Cover Image Prompt

## Subject
A professional infographic cover representing a factory visit checklist for Australian businesses sourcing from China. The image communicates trust, verification, and professional B2B partnership.

**Zone 1 (Left — Factory Core):**
- Stylized factory building with industrial silhouette (chimneys, loading docks)
- Overlaid with a verification badge showing checkmark
- Subtle gear/cog motifs suggesting manufacturing quality

**Zone 2 (Center — Checklist Theme):**
- Large clipboard or document icon with visible checklist
- 3-4 checkmark items suggested (not full text, just visual rhythm)
- Clipboard styled in navy with amber accent

**Zone 3 (Right — Verification & Trust):**
- Shield or badge graphic representing SAMR/business licence verification
- Small certificate/document icon
- Location pin icon suggesting factory address verification

**Zone 4 (Bottom — Accent):**
- Subtle map grid pattern or coordinates
- Small globe or Australia-China relationship indicator (subtle, not literal)
