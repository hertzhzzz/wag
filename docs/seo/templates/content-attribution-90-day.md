## Route migration boundary

`public-route-v1` replaces historical free-form path capture. Existing records containing raw paths are not automatically promoted, copied, hashed, or reclassified because doing so could preserve personal, query-like, private, or semantic data. Recapture an eligible touch through an exact allowlisted public route and emit only its route ID. Email-shaped segments, telephone-shaped segments, query strings, fragments, unknown slugs, and private routes must be rejected rather than migrated.

## Reporting boundary

Reports may show first-touch and assisted-touch views separately by approved campaign, cluster, article, and funnel values. They must show unavailable data as unavailable, not as zero, and must not infer causation, qualification, ranking, or revenue from an attribution touch alone.

A record is not eligible for actual reporting when consent, privacy review, allowlist integrity, data-mode separation, timestamp validity, storage integrity, or enquiry-join validation is unresolved.

## Anonymous session and privacy metadata

- Anonymous session ID (`session_<high-entropy-opaque-id>`):
- Consent state: `granted` | `denied` | `unavailable`
- Consent version: `seo-attribution-consent-v1`
- Purpose: `content-to-enquiry-attribution`
- Retention: `90` days, matching the attribution window

The session ID is an opaque first-party correlation value. It is not a browser fingerprint, custom device ID, CRM identifier, email-derived value, or lead-quality signal. Do not collect or persist names, email addresses, phone numbers, company names, addresses, message text, product details, query strings, user-agent-derived attributes, or browser/device data for the purpose of inferring lead quality. Lead quality is a human-review or server-owned field outside this contract.

Actual storage is allowed only when consent is `granted`. `denied` and `unavailable` fail soft and must not block enquiry submission. `dry_run` may preview a deterministic record with explicit metadata but must perform zero browser-storage reads/writes and must not emit analytics payloads for non-granted consent.

## Server-side enquiry join boundary

The join API requires `scope: server-reporting`. The server/reporting layer joins the opaque `enquiryId` after the enquiry API confirms success; the browser does not decide success, qualification, revenue, or lead quality. The join projection may contain only the opaque enquiry ID, approved touch projections, timestamps, mode, and the server-reporting scope. It must not expose `sessionId`, privacy metadata, form payloads, or any contact details. Missing scope, malformed IDs, mode mismatch, future timestamps, fixture leakage, and expired journeys fail closed.
