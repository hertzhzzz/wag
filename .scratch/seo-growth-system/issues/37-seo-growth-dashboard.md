# 37 — Extend the SEO Growth Dashboard

**What to build:** Deliver a decision dashboard that connects governed content health, search visibility, GEO observations, article-graph quality, evidence review, and consent-aware enquiry attribution. Every rate must retain its raw counts, definition, date range, and source so operators can distinguish movement from measurement gaps.

**Blocked by:** 27, 30, 36.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Search and enquiry rates display numerator, denominator, date range, and comparison period.
- [ ] Qualified organic touches, successful enquiries, and assisted enquiries are available by governed cluster.
- [ ] GEO trends show brand mention, owned citation, accuracy, completeness, and competitor observations.
- [ ] Indexation, graph, evidence, review, and validation health are visible as separate operational signals.
- [ ] Dashboard definitions and source lineage are accessible for every metric.
- [ ] Missing, delayed, or partial data is labeled rather than converted to zero.
- [ ] Dashboard rendering performs no content generation, publication, deployment, or indexing action.
